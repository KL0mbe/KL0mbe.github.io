---
title: Migrate Your Damn DB's
quote: Do not leave it to hindsight to wisen you up
date: 2026-09-15
---

"To forego migration is to forego an ever needed skill, in favor of a moments ease"

Let me paint you a picture. Your working on a personal project, just scribbling along, 
doing a fine and dandy job and using sql of course because your not insane.

Then as you move along you realize that one table you thought was done really needs 
another column, and actually that column shouldnt hold a string it should probably be 
an int and hold a FK to another table.

So you do what we all do and `DROP SCHEMA public CASCADE;` write you new schema, 
pop it in and re seed. But this is a luxury; not taken for granted but taken advantage of. 
It is not that theres anything wrong with quickly dumping your bad schema and popping 
in the updated one. It is that, that will never happen in production ever. You know that 
when your out actually putting in the work you will have to migrate to the new schema 
to maintain the data in the DB.

Why not take advantage of the oppertunity to put in the kilometers and migrate your 
tables when you come across your own incompetence in writing a schema. Because you 
don't get better at running by driving to the finish line, you don't improve your 
cooking by ordering take out and you dont learn to migrate by dropping your tables.

just Migrate Your Damn DB's and prep yourself for the real world. To forego migration is 
to forego an ever needed skill, in favor of a moments ease. And it will be but a moment,
because if you ever do end up dropping tables in the real world. You will find that most 
companies will be just as quick to drop the slacker who did it.
